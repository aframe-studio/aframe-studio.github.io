About Me

Hey, I'm Affan. Nice to Meet You!

Hi, I'm Affan — an independent UGC creator from Karnataka, India.

I create engaging video ads and product visuals for brands looking to showcase their products through creative storytelling.

From beauty and skincare to fragrance, fashion, technology, lifestyle, fitness, and everyday products, I enjoy exploring different creative styles to develop appealing product-focused content.

My focus is on creating short-form advertising concepts that capture attention, communicate product features, and feel natural on social media.

I'm open to collaborating with brands worldwide and developing creative concepts tailored to their products and audiences.
